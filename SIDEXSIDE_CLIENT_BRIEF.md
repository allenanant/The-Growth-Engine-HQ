## What Is SideXSide?

SideXSide is a **privacy-first campus safety companion app** designed for U.S. colleges and universities. Think of it as a safety layer that sits alongside students throughout their campus life — without ever tracking them, collecting their data, or compromising their privacy.

**The one-liner:** A campus safety app that protects students without spying on them.

**Why it matters:** Every university in the U.S. is legally required to prioritize campus safety (Clery Act). Most current solutions rely on GPS tracking, invasive data collection, or clunky panic-button systems. SideXSide flips that — it delivers real safety features with **zero tracking and zero data collection**. That's the competitive edge.

## Business Model — B2B White-Label SaaS

**This is 100% a B2B play.** We are NOT selling to students. We sell to universities, and universities deploy it to their students under their own brand.

### How It Works
1. **We license SideXSide to universities** as a white-label SaaS product
2. **The university brands it** as their own campus safety app (their logo, their colors, their name)
3. **Students download** what looks like their university's own safety app
4. **University pays us** a licensing fee (recurring monthly/annual)

### Why White-Label?
- Universities want to show students they built something — not that they bought something
- White-labeling removes the "third-party app" objection
- Increases adoption because it feels native to the campus ecosystem
- Creates stickiness — once it's branded and deployed, switching costs are high

### Revenue Model
- B2B SaaS licensing (recurring revenue per university)
- Target: 150-200 U.S. universities
- Focus: Mid-size schools with 10,000-30,000+ students

## Sales Strategy — Who We're Selling To

We've identified **5 decision-maker personas** within each university. You need to understand all of them because different schools have different buying structures.

### The 5 Decision-Maker Personas

**1. VP of Student Affairs / Dean of Students**
- **Role:** Owns the student experience, campus life, and safety programs
- **Pain point:** Needs to show the board they're proactive about safety
- **Pitch angle:** "Deploy a safety solution students actually want to use — because it respects their privacy"

**2. Chief Information Officer (CIO) / IT Director**
- **Role:** Evaluates and approves all campus technology
- **Pain point:** Doesn't want another app that creates data liability
- **Pitch angle:** "Zero data collection means zero data breach risk for your IT department"

**3. Campus Police / Public Safety Director**
- **Role:** Manages on-the-ground safety operations
- **Pain point:** Existing tools have low student adoption
- **Pitch angle:** "Students actually use this because they trust it — no tracking means no resistance"

**4. Title IX Coordinator**
- **Role:** Ensures compliance with federal safety and equity requirements
- **Pain point:** Needs demonstrable safety infrastructure for audits
- **Pitch angle:** "SideXSide gives you documented safety infrastructure without the privacy controversy"

**5. University President / Provost**
- **Role:** Final budget approval and strategic direction
- **Pain point:** Reputation and liability
- **Pitch angle:** "Be the university known for protecting students AND their privacy — that's a press story, not just a safety tool"

### Sales Cycle Expectation
- University procurement is slow — expect 3-6 month sales cycles
- Goal: Get pilot programs started (low-risk for the university, high-value for us)
- Once 2-3 pilots succeed, we use case studies to accelerate the next wave
